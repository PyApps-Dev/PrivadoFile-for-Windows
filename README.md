# PrivadoFile™

PrivadoFile is a modern desktop utility suite engineered by Quantum Software™ [(Github 1](https://github.com/PyDevYoussef) [Github 2](https://github.com/QuantumSoftwareGroup) [Github 3)](https://github.com/quantumsoftwaresand) adapted to Windows os by [PyApps](https://github.com/PyApps-dev), to secure, manage and protect all your personal and confidential data. Designed for pure local performance, PrivadoFile operates completely offline to ensure maximum privacy and full data ownership. 

## 🚀 Features

Here are the tools available in the suite:

-   **CryptoFile™:** Encrypt and decrypt your files using high-grade security to safeguard your sensitive data from any unauthorized access.
-   **DestroFile™:** Permanently shred and wipe files or folders to make them completely unrecoverable.
-   **MetaFile™:** Inspect, edit, or strip metadata from your files to protect your personal information before sharing.
-   **SafeFile™:** Keep your sensitive files strictly private by securing them inside a dedicated local vault on your machine.
